Drag a gradient straight onto the photograph. Darken a sky, lift a subject out of shadow, warm one corner — and the mask stays where you put it when you crop, straighten or turn the frame.

Color that is
right, and
checkable.

Orion used to render blue skies violet. The fix was not a nicer-looking number — it was building the color stage the DNG specification actually describes, then testing it against the camera's own rendering of the same frame.

Why you can trust the picture

Every filter cites
a published paper.

Not a slogan — a rule the code is held to. Each non-trivial filter names the research it implements, with the venue and the year. Where something is not yet sourced, that is written down too, in public.

  • Haze removalHe, Sun & Tang · CVPR 2009
  • Local contrastParis, Hasinoff & Kautz · SIGGRAPH 2011
  • Shadow recoveryHessel & Morel · WACV 2020
  • Film looksAdobe Cube LUT spec · 2013
  • Mask falloffPerlin · ACM TOG 2002

Your edits are
yours to take.

Settings are written beside the photograph in plain XMP — the same format every other editor reads. Nothing is locked in a database only Orion can open. Delete the app tomorrow and your raw files are untouched.
