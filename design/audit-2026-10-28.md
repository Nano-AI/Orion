## 4. Where motion is missing (`find-animation-opportunities`)

| # | Where | Today | Purpose | Frequency | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Slider fill and thumb | Teleport on reset, Auto, undo, preset | State indication | Occasional | Spring, damping 1.0, response 0.3 s, from the on-screen value; none during a drag |
| 2 | Zoom via Fit / 100% pill or double-click | Snaps | Spatial consistency | Occasional | Spring about the click point, damping 1.0, response 0.35 s; Cmd+0/9 jump (keyboard) |
| 3 | Pinch and pan release | n/a | Gesture seam | Occasional | Momentum projection (decel 0.998) and rubber-band at limits, spring back damping 1.0 |
| 4 | Compare divider release | Stops dead | Gesture seam | Occasional | Angle snap to 0/45/90° by spring with release velocity |
| 5 | Mask overlay on select | Instant | State indication | Occasional | Opacity 0 → set, 150 ms ease-out |
| 6 | Histogram bins arrival | Pops in, reflows | Preventing a jarring change | Every swap | No reflow; bins fade 100 ms; placeholder pulse only if empty past 500 ms |
| 7 | Status-line messages | Appear instantly | Visibility | Occasional | Fade 150 ms ease-out |

**Rejected**

- The photo crossfade on ← →: keyboard-driven, hundreds a day, so never animated.
- Filmstrip scroll or ring motion on browse: same reason.
- A histogram curve morphing between photos: data being read, and high frequency.
- Thumb motion during a drag: direct manipulation is 1:1.
- A staggered gallery entrance: a functional grid opened often.

**Verdict.** The shell needs little motion, and what it has is right. The
leverage is in rows 1-3: they make the instrument answer the hand. Highest
single item: the slider glide. Everything here is assigned in round 2 (u2, u4,
u5); the final review re-runs these four skills on the merged result.
